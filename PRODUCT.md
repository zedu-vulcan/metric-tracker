# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

In-house marketing teams using one shared workspace to continuously optimize their content, SEO, social, and paid-media performance.

## Product Purpose

Amp brings the marketing metrics that signal momentum and effectiveness into one operational dashboard. Its purpose is to help a team notice meaningful changes, identify where to act, and improve performance continuously rather than assembling retrospective reports.

## Operating Context

Teams return to the dashboard during their normal marketing cadence to check content output and reading behavior, organic-search health, social performance, and paid-ad efficiency. They compare current periods with prior periods and move from an aggregate signal to the channel or metric needing attention.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Tracks content metrics: articles/posts published, average read time, and backlinks.
- Tracks SEO metrics: organic traffic, keyword rankings, impressions, and click-through rate.
- Tracks social metrics: followers gained, posts published, impressions, and engagements.
- Tracks paid-ad metrics including spend, impressions, and clicks.
- The existing project is a Next.js web application with NextAuth and Prisma.
- Exact data connections, reporting cadence controls, roles, alerts, and metric definitions remain open product decisions.

## Brand Commitments

The product is called **Amp**, a multi-tenant marketing workspace (it started as Zedu's). Each team works in its own project. The primary brand color is `#7D68F9`, the navigation uses deep Zedu violet (`#24145F`), and the mark is Zedu's speech bubble with a lightning bolt.

## Evidence on Hand

No verified customer data, testimonials, benchmark claims, or production dashboard data are available. Any interface examples should be clearly presented as illustrative or synthetic.

## Product Principles

1. Make change visible before making data exhaustive.
2. Keep cross-channel comparison legible without flattening meaningful differences.
3. Turn a signal into a clear next investigation.
4. Favor calm, repeatable scanning over report-like spectacle.
5. Treat trustworthy context—time range, comparison, source, and status—as part of every metric.
